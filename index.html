03

NASA Space Apps 2026 · team lead of 3 Due 15 Nov 2026

Planet X

Since 2025, NASA's SPHEREx mission has mapped the entire sky every six months in 102 bands of near-infrared light — over a billion objects, many of which give themselves away by shifting position between passes. Comets, asteroids, brown dwarfs, whole new planets. We're building the public web tool that lets anyone see that movement without a research account.
